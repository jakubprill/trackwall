Trackwall 0.5.4 lets you copy from a session's terminal the way Copilot CLI suggests, keeps a task's terminal large with its notices folded into one line, and lets the supervisor resume tasks.

## Fixed

- **Copy from a session.** Copilot CLI selects text when you drag over it and copies it on Ctrl+C or a right click, but nothing reached the clipboard. It does now. Dragging with Option (macOS) or Shift (Windows) held down still works too. See [Sessions](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/sessions).
- **First start on Windows.** The window no longer stays washed over with "Connecting to the daemon…" after it first shows, until you resize or click it.

## What's new

- **More room for a task's terminal.** The summary, verification and warnings above a task's tabs are folded into one line of notices, such as "Summary" or "Verification failed"; click one, or "Details", to see the sections. In the grid, a task's tile shows a badge next to its title (yellow with the number of warnings), and a new warning lies over the terminal as one line with "Show", without changing its size. See [Agent tasks](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/tasks).
- **Trusting a config tells the agent.** When an agent was turned away because a mise config was not trusted yet, Trackwall tells it once you press "Trust this config", and it runs its mise task again without you having to ask. See [mise tasks](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/mise-tasks).
- **The supervisor resumes tasks.** Ask the supervisor to continue a task whose session no longer runs, for example after Trackwall restarts: it starts the task again in its own worktree and conversation, the same as "Resume". See [Supervisor](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/supervisor).

## Moving from 0.5.3

Click "Update and restart" when Trackwall offers 0.5.4, or install it by hand ([Update by hand](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/guide/install#update)). Your data in `~/.trackwall` stays. Sessions running when you restart end with it; resume their tasks with "Resume" or ask the supervisor.

## Known limitations

- Sessions run Copilot CLI only, and it has to be installed and signed in.
- The app is not signed: on Windows download it with `gh` or `curl.exe`, or run `Unblock-File` on the zip; on macOS remove the quarantine with `xattr`.
- A proxy that signs you in with your Windows account or replaces certificates can stop the update download; the manual update still works then.
- macOS on Apple Silicon only.
