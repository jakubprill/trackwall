Trackwall 0.5.6 starts cleanly on Windows, lets you run a session without a worktree, gives sessions the environment from your mise config, and adds a doctor and an error journal to new, grouped Settings.

## Fixed

- **"The daemon was not started" on Windows.** Every launch on Windows showed this error until you clicked "Try again". The window now waits for the daemon to start and shows an error only when the start really fails.
- **Several approvals in one turn.** When the agent runs several commands at once, the session keeps asking for approval until you have answered all of them, and the panel shows the next request as soon as the terminal does. See [Approvals](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/approvals).

## What's new

- **Sessions without a worktree.** In "Sessions > +", uncheck "In its own worktree" to open Copilot CLI in a repo's own working copy, or pick "No repo: the projects folder" for a free session tied to no repo. The form warns you when another session already works in the same folder. See [Sessions](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/sessions).
- **Tasks without approvals.** Tick "Run without asking for approvals" when you start a task, or let the supervisor suggest it on a proposal, and its agent works without stopping for you; its header says "No approvals". Your `toolPermissions` deny rules still refuse what they name. See [Agent tasks](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/tasks).
- **Environment from mise.** Every session gets the `[env]` of your mise config, so one `mise.toml` in your projects folder can set tokens and settings for every repo, task and the supervisor. Secrets stay encrypted on disk, and the agent's shell and local MCP servers get only the ones you name in `agentSecrets`. See [Environment from mise](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/mise-environment).
- **Settings in categories.** Settings lists Workspace, Repo hosts, Agents, App and Diagnostics on the left; the Daemon view moved into Diagnostics. See [Navigation and windows](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/navigation-and-windows).
- **Doctor.** "Settings > Diagnostics" checks your projects folder, Git, Copilot CLI and its trusted folder, mise, and gh or glab, and says what to do about anything missing. See [Doctor](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/doctor).
- **Trackwall errors.** Trackwall keeps its own errors on your computer, anonymized, counted once each with where in its code they came from, under "Settings > Diagnostics". Nothing is sent anywhere. See [Trackwall errors](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/trackwall-errors).
- **A repo at a glance.** A repo opens on its card, which now starts with "At a glance", four lines that sum it up; "In this repo" under the header lists its proposals, sessions, tasks and running mise tasks, one click from each. See [Repos](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/repos).
- **A supervisor panel that is easier to read.** Widen the panel with the arrows in its header, and the supervisor answers short with the conclusion first. Session terminals use Trackwall's own colours and blend into the window. See [Supervisor](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/supervisor).
- **A clearer Home.** Every repo has a label with what is going on in it, and "Settings > App > Home scene" switches between holograms and lighter orbs for a slow graphics card. While you do not touch it, the scene moves more slowly so it does not slow down your sessions. See [Home](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/home).

## Moving from 0.5.5

Click "Update and restart" (or "Update after their turn" while agents work) when Trackwall offers 0.5.6, or install it by hand ([Update by hand](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/guide/install#update)). Your data in `~/.trackwall` stays, and the sessions that were running come back after the restart. Sessions now get the environment from mise; if a session lacks a variable it had before, check the line under its header.

## Known limitations

- Sessions run Copilot CLI only, and it has to be installed and signed in.
- The app is not signed: on Windows download it with `gh` or `curl.exe`, or run `Unblock-File` on the zip; on macOS remove the quarantine with `xattr`.
- A proxy that signs you in with your Windows account or replaces certificates can stop the update download; the manual update still works then.
- macOS on Apple Silicon only.
