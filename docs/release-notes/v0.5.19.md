Trackwall 0.5.19 shows running services on the graph and Home, opens services from a dev container in your browser, lets you take files out of a shell, and shows what each session's conversation used.

## Before you start

If Trackwall runs in a dev container, forward port 7701 next to 7700 in `devcontainer.json` (`"forwardPorts": [7700, 7701]`) and rebuild the container, so that "Open" on a service works. See [Dev container](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/guide/dev-container).

## What's new

- **Open a service from a dev container.** "Open" on a ready service shows it in your browser also when Trackwall runs in a dev container, through Trackwall's service proxy on the port after its own: `http://shop.localhost:7701/` for the repo `shop`. The proxy reaches only services Trackwall started and found ready, and refuses what pages of other sites send to them. See [Services](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/services).
- **Running services on the graph and Home.** A repo whose service runs shows a green dot and its port on the connection graph, or its state, such as "starting" or "crashed"; Home shows it as a ring on the floor and in the repo's label. While a task runs its copy of the service on the repo's port, they show that copy. See [Connection graph](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/graph) and [Home](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/home).
- **Files on disk in the Shell tab.** "Files on disk" above a shell lists its folder as it is now, also build output and files you have not committed, and a click on a file downloads it, also out of a container. See [Shell](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/shell).
- **A shell in the projects folder.** "Shell" next to the projects folder's path in "Repos" opens a shell in the folder with all your repos. See [Shell](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/shell).
- **What a session used.** Every session that has talked to the model shows what its conversation used so far, as Copilot CLI's `/usage` counts it: AI credits, premium requests, or tokens with your own model provider. The task's header shows it too, and "Sessions" shows the sum. See [Sessions](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/sessions).
- **Star repos.** A star on a repo in "Repos" keeps it at the top of the list, in the app and in a browser tab. See [Repos](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/repos).
- **One background under session terminals.** In "Sessions", every terminal, alone or in a grid tile, sits on the same background as the rest of the window. See [Sessions](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/sessions).
- **Line actions in the conversation's diff panel (preview).** The button in front of a line offers "Comment", which adds a review comment as in "Changes", "Open in VS Code" at that line, and "Ask about this", which quotes the line in your message. See [Agent tasks](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/tasks).
- **Answer an approval with the keyboard in the conversation view (preview).** The approval request takes the keyboard when it shows, unless you are writing a message: ↑ and ↓ move between its answers and Enter picks one. See [Supervisor](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/supervisor).
- **Shorter answers from the connection tools.** `who_calls`, `subgraph_for` and `find_endpoint` answer agents with short text instead of JSON, so they cost the agent less of its context. See [MCP](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/features/mcp).

## Moving from 0.5.18

Click "Update and restart" (or "Update after their turn" while agents work) when Trackwall offers 0.5.19, or install it by hand ([Update by hand](https://jakubprill.github.io/trackwall/guide/install#update)). Your data in `~/.trackwall` stays, and the sessions that were running come back after the restart. In a dev container, read "Before you start" above.

## Known limitations

- Sessions run Copilot CLI only, and it has to be installed and signed in.
- The app is not signed: on Windows download it with `gh` or `curl.exe`, or run `Unblock-File` on the zip; on macOS remove the quarantine with `xattr`.
- A proxy that signs you in with your Windows account or replaces certificates can stop the update download; the manual update still works then.
- macOS on Apple Silicon only.
- Chat (preview) has no Java navigation for tasks, and a running task cannot switch between the terminal and Chat.
