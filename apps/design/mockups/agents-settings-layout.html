Agents settings layout

Today Agents is one column of seven sections, about three and a half screens tall: the Copilot CLI setup with the list of every tool, four settings, Copilot usage with two top-10 lists, and the MCP call list. The column is 576 px wide in a window three times as wide. Three ways to bring the usage report and the calls within one screen.

Variant A · chosen and built (ADR-212)

A new “Usage” category beside Agents

Agents keeps what you set; Usage holds what agents used and did, wide, with the two lists side by side and the calls as a dense table in their own scroll box.
