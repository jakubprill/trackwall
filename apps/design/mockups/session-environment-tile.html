How it works

  • A 🔑 n chip in the header, next to the task's notice badge. The number counts every variable from mise. Sessions without a task have it too (supervisor, free session, session in a repo).
  • A yellow chip when the session got less than its place gives (an untrusted mise.toml, a missing age key). Then it shows at once, without hovering.
  • Hovering shows the card after a moment, clicking pins it (also from the keyboard: Enter, Esc closes). The card lies over the terminal, so the terminal does not resize.
  • Three groups in the card: what the agent's tools get (agentSecrets), what is hidden from them (struck through), the rest. Names only, never values.

Rejected

  • A browser tooltip (title) alone, as in the single session view: it shows late, cannot be scrolled or reached from the keyboard, and with 20 variables it is unreadable.
  • A bar under the tile header: takes a terminal row in every tile, and in a grid of 4 every row counts.
  • Folding it into the notice badge: that badge is only for tasks and mixes warnings with information you check on demand.
