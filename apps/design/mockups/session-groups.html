See which sessions belong together

A session that proposes a task, whose session proposes another, makes a chain. The list already puts the chain under its main session, but an indent of one step and a thin grey line are easy to miss, and the grid shows nothing of it. Here every group (a main session and everything under it) gets its own colour, in the list and on its tiles, and one click shows only that group in the grid.

List variant
Layout
Hover a row and click ⊞ group, or pick it from a tile's menu: the grid shows that group only. Click a row outside the filter: the filter goes away, so the selected entry is always on screen.

1. A colour for each group

  1. A group is a main session with every task and proposal under it, at any depth. A session alone has no colour, so colour always means "these belong together".
  2. Stripe (recommended): a coloured bar along each row of the group, and the level as ↳1, ↳2 next to the name, since a third level no longer moves right.
  3. Frame: one coloured outline around the whole group. Clearer at a glance, but it costs a little width and nests badly with the selected row.
  4. In the grid a tile of a group has a coloured top edge and says what it sits under (under payments), so a chain reads even when its tiles are apart.
  5. Four colours that are not state colours (violet, pink, blue, coral), given in list order and reused after four groups. A group keeps its colour while it is on the list.

2. Only this group in the grid

  1. A row of a group has ⊞ group on hover, and a tile's menu has "Only this group". The grid switches to Auto with the group's entries only, in list order.
  2. The layout bar shows the filter as a chip in the group's colour with an × to clear it. The list stays full; rows outside the group dim.
  3. Selecting an entry outside the group clears the filter. Numbers and Alt+digit keep pointing at list positions.
  4. For a second monitor, a new window (already there) with this filter does the job of a "workspace" without saving one.
  5. In code: the group and its colour index from sessionRows in lib/session-entries.ts, the filter in lib/session-grid.ts, both with unit tests; four colour tokens in index.css. No daemon change.
