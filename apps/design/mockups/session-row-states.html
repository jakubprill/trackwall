The state words

  • Needs you: answer / approval / plan was waiting for you, asking for approval, waiting for plan approval.
  • Ready for review is a task handed over for review while its agent is idle, ended or gone; it replaces the "In review" mark.
  • Working, Idle, Error, Finished, Stopped, Paused: the same states, capitalised.
  • A hollow dot is a guess from the screen, not from a hook; it was the word "probably".
  • Verify again was "Verify outdated": the files changed since the last verdict.
