Tips and What's new

Trackwall teaches its features in place. A tip shows under a control the first time it is on screen, one at a time in a window, and goes for good with "Got it". After an update, a strip above the views lists the changes worth a look, each with "Show", which opens the place it lives in and shows its tip there.
