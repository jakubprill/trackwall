Trackwall // design gallery

Every mockup in one place

The mockups made before building Trackwall's views, each in the app's own tokens, with the variants that were proposed and what was chosen. A mockup is a design record: it shows the proposal as it was decided, and the app may have moved on since. Open a card to click through the mockup.

Tokens

One dark theme (ADR-16). Cyan marks what is active; green, yellow and red speak only of state; the four group colours only tie related sessions together. The values are those of packages/ui/src/index.css.
